AI Use-Case Prioritizer

Decide which AI and automation ideas to fund first, and define how each one fails and when you will kill it, before anyone writes a line of code. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

1. What matters to your team?

Set how much each factor counts (0 to 10). The ranking updates live. Effort and risk count in your favor when they are low.

2. Your ideas

Score each idea from 1 to 5, then answer the two questions most scorecards skip: how could this fail, and what result would make you stop?

3. Results

Ranked by weighted score (0 to 100). Click a dot on the matrix to jump to that idea.

4. One-pager

A summary you can send to a sponsor. Print it to PDF or copy it as Markdown.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your ideas are saved on this device only (and in a share link if you create one). MIT licensed.
